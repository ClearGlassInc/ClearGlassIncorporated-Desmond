## Reusable production card

For every item: **brief → source verification → draft → claim/privacy/accessibility/rights review → named approval → scheduled publish → capture URL → 7-day observation → 30-day decision**. Alt text must convey purpose; videos require accurate captions and a transcript; graphics must meet contrast requirements. Comments are moderated for safety and privacy, not manufactured engagement.

## Approval and stop rules

The editor rejects duplicated local pages, fabricated anecdotes, unsourced statistics, copyrighted event imagery, undisclosed paid relationships, personal information, security-sensitive client detail, and claims that ClearGlassInc Artemis is affiliated with an organization. Pause scheduled posts during an incident or when facts become stale. Correct transparently; retain the original, correction reason, approver, and timestamp in the audit record.
