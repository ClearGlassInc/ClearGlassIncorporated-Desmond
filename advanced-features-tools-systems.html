The safety model

Every action earns its route

governance.py scores each proposed action from 0 to 100 and routes it by tier. The invariant is simple and enforced in code: no high or critical action can execute until an approvals row reaches approved. The self-check in daily_loop.py — and the governance tests — fail by design if that door is ever left open.

Operating rules the agents can't override: never fabricate inventory, reviews, sales, or urgency; never move live pricing, tax, payment, refund or fulfillment without approval; log every action.
