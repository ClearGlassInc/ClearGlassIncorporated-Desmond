PERSONNEL / CYBER / DOMESTIC

Personnel and sustainment

Measure application → offer → enrolment → training → occupational qualification → retained effective strength. Track instructor capacity, maintenance backlog, spares, depot throughput, contractor reliance and lifecycle cost. Recruitment is a leading indicator, not usable capability.

Defensive cyber

Prioritize identity, segmentation, secure logs, detection, vulnerability/third-party risk, immutable backup, exercised recovery and incident coordination. Model output is untrusted; no exploitation or targeting features.

Domestic preparedness

Volume shocks, organized crime, wildfire, flood, outage and cyber events create concurrent demand. Rights-respecting needs include privacy-preserving workflow, resource visibility, public alerts, auditable handoffs, minimization, retention control and appeal.

Interoperability

Compatible equipment is insufficient: shared identity, semantics, releasability policy, training, exercises and sustainment determine effective coalition operations.

SCENARIO

Signal → decision → governed lesson.

  1. Public weather/ice advice and authorized infrastructure telemetry arrive with markings, timestamps and hashes.
  2. Foundry validates and maps observations; fact stays distinct from inference.
  3. AIP drafts a continuity assessment with corroboration but cannot task assets.
  4. Policy requires approval for interagency coordination. An operator corrects a stale dependency and rejects the proposed priority.
  5. The minimized correction and outcome become an eval case—not an autonomous goal change.
  6. A recency-check prompt patch passes offline and shadow safety/quality gates.
  7. An independent owner approves a bounded canary; Apollo records the version and rollback. Any drift or policy failure reverts.
