# Phase 3: Paid Domination

## Objective

Use paid distribution to amplify proven organic content and build useful video-view audiences. Do not spend for cinematic appearance alone.

## Readiness gate

Before activation, record the Meta Business account, ad account, Page, Pixel ID, domain verification, privacy notice, consent mechanism, named budget approver, and rollback owner. `assets/js/meta-pixel-consent.js` is inert until a consent interface calls `ClearGlassMetaPixel.grant(pixelId)`. A Pixel ID is public configuration, but it must not be invented.

Customer-list data must not enter GitHub, Actions artifacts, logs, issue text, or the content agent. Upload only contacts whose collection and intended advertising use have been reviewed for consent and applicable Canadian privacy/anti-spam obligations. Use Meta's secured interface and its required hashing process.

## Campaigns

1. Seeding: publish five Reels organically. When a Reel exceeds 50% completion within six hours, propose a CAD 10/day Engagement test using Advantage+ Audience and Advantage+ Placements.
2. Profile Domination: retarget consented three-second video viewers with a Feed carousel. Slide 1: “Why clarity is the product.” Slide 2: Artemis. Slide 3: Founder story. CTA: Follow Profile.
3. Lead Magnet: “Compliant vs Secure: 7-Gap Audit for Canadian SMBs.” Collect the minimum email data with clear consent, purpose, retention, and unsubscribe language before any follow-up or Facebook invitation.

## Decision policy

- Pause recommendation: hook retention below 30% after at least CAD 5 spend.
- Scale recommendation: paid hook retention at or above 30%, capped at CAD 20/day.
- Every create, pause, scale, audience upload, or publish action requires human approval in Meta Ads Manager.
- Treat Meta's product-wide benchmark claims as context, not a ClearGlass forecast. Use ClearGlass's own controlled test results for decisions.

## Audit record

For every decision retain Reel ID, version, objective, audience settings, placements, spend, impressions, three-second views, hook retention definition, completion definition, test window, recommendation, approver, and resulting platform action. Never store contact identifiers in the audit record.
