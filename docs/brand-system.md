# Brand System

ClearGlass is positioned as Burlington's evidence-first cybersecurity and AI-risk partner: practical, executive-readable, local, measurable, and transparent. Use premium glass UI, high contrast, disciplined futuristic details, restrained neon accents, accessible controls, and precise hierarchy.

Forbidden claims: guaranteed security, unhackable systems, government affiliation, military capability, fake customers, fake testimonials, unverifiable certifications, fabricated partnerships, or invented campaign results.
