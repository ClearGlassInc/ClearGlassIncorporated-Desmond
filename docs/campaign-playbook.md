# Campaign Playbook

Initial packages live in `data/campaigns/initial-burlington-campaign-packages.json` and remain drafts until human approval.

## Burlington Cyber Risk Checkup
Audience: Burlington businesses with 5-100 employees. Offer: Executive Cyber and AI Risk Checkup. CTA: Book a confidential risk review. Deliverables: landing page, search ads, LinkedIn ads, five LinkedIn posts, three outreach emails, lead magnet, conversion form, follow-up workflow, and measurement plan.

## Microsoft 365 Account Protection
Audience: law, accounting, real-estate, and professional-services firms. Message: one compromised administrator account can expose the organization. Deliverables: industry pages, search ads, checklist, CTA, retargeting copy, and email sequence.

## Ransomware Readiness for Burlington SMEs
Audience: manufacturing, logistics, construction, and property management. Message: backups are not evidence of recovery until restoration has been tested. Deliverables: scorecard, assessment offer, article, webinar outline, ad set, and referral-partner package.

## Nonprofit Cyber Resilience
Audience: Burlington and Halton nonprofits. Offer: affordable security workshop and readiness assessment. Deliverables: landing page, partnership email, deck outline, registration flow, sponsorship proposal, and follow-up campaign.

## AI Governance for Ontario Businesses
Audience: organizations using generative AI without formal controls. Offer: AI-use policy, risk register, governance assessment, and executive briefing. Deliverables: lead magnet, landing page, LinkedIn campaign, email sequence, questionnaire, and recurring governance-retainer offer.
