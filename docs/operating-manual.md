# Operating Manual

1. Review market brief sources.
2. Draft or update campaign package.
3. Run claim verification and tests.
4. Submit publication, outreach, or ad launch to approval queue.
5. Export approved assets manually to ad/email platforms.
6. Import measured outcomes.
7. Review weekly revenue metrics.
8. Approve or reject optimization recommendations.

Manual requirements remain for legal review, ad account connection, spend approval, personal-data collection, procurement contact, and publication.
