It fuses public environmental signals with dependency context, converts observations into GREEN/YELLOW/RED operating bands, and produces evidence-backed recommendations for monitoring, validation, or escalation. The value is not another dashboard; it is an auditable decision layer that shows what is exposed, why it matters now, which action is justified, and where human approval is required before any external claim or client notification.
