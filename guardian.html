Response Playbook

Incident Response Playbook Matrix

A disciplined, repeatable response sequence — each stage moves the incident toward containment, evidence, and hardening.

01

Detect

Surface the signal early through monitoring, alerts, and reported anomalies.

02

Triage

Assess scope, severity, and business impact to set the response priority.

03

Contain

Limit spread and isolate affected systems to stop further escalation.

04

Preserve Evidence

Capture logs, artifacts, and timestamps before they can be lost.

05

Escalate

Notify the right owners and approvers through defined escalation paths.

06

Review

Analyze root cause and confirm the incident is fully understood.

07

Report

Document findings and communicate clearly to stakeholders and executives.

08

Harden

Close the gaps and strengthen controls to prevent recurrence.
