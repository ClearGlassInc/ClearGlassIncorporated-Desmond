DANGER ZONES

Red flags that
waste your time.

At your rate, 1 bad client costs you $8k–$15k in opportunity cost. Recognize these patterns before you sign anything.

🪤
Time Trap

Open-Scope Fixed-Price

"We'll pay $2k for the whole project" with no defined scope. You'll deliver 10× what's worth that price. Always define deliverables before price. If they resist scope definition, they'll resist paying you too.

🧪
Free Work Disguised

The "Test Project"

Any unpaid "trial" or "small test" from a stranger is free labour extraction. Legitimate clients with budget don't ask for free samples. If they want to see your work, send your case studies and the Loom demo.

📉
Rate Anchoring

Low-Rate Platform Traps

Fiverr, PeoplePerHour, and freelancer.com flood the market with $15/hr competition. Your expertise cannot compete on price there — it competes on outcome. Stick to Toptal, Arc.dev, Contra, and direct outreach.

🤝
Equity Bait

"We'll Give You Equity Instead"

Strangers offering equity for your labour are paying with fictional currency. Equity from a pre-revenue startup is worth $0 today. If a company genuinely wants a tech co-founder relationship, it starts with a conversation — not "build this for free."

🔒
IP Risk

NDA-Resistors

Any client who won't sign a mutual NDA before sharing their systems or strategy is either hiding something or doesn't respect the professional relationship. Your Artemis/Guardian IP must be protected — always use mutual NDAs.

⏰
Scope Creep

The Endless Requester

"While you're at it, can you also..." is the most expensive sentence in freelancing. Every contract must include a change-order clause: new requests beyond scope get a separate quote within 24 hours. No exceptions.
