Questions, answered plainly.

Will ClearGlass change anything?

No. The Quick-Audit is read-only. Remediation is separately scoped, approved and logged.

Is this a penetration test?

No. There is no exploitation, evasion or intrusive scanning. Material needs outside the scope are referred to a separate authorized engagement.

Does this certify compliance?

No. It is not a certification, assurance opinion, legal opinion or compliance attestation.

What if evidence is unavailable?

The report marks the control as not verified and explains what evidence would be needed. It does not convert missing evidence into a failure or a pass.
