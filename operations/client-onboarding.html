Client Onboarding Pipeline

ClearGlass Inc. uses a controlled onboarding sequence: qualify, contract, bill, activate, and review.

Pipeline

StageExit criteriaOwner
QualifiedBudget, decision-maker, need, and timing confirmed.Founder
ContractAgreement sent and accepted.Founder
PaymentDeposit or first invoice paid.Finance
ActivationKickoff call complete and delivery workspace opened.Delivery lead
ReviewQuarterly review and renewal decision logged.Founder

Control rule

No contract, no kickoff. No payment, no access. No signed scope, no custom work.
