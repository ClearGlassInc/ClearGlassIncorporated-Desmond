# OSIT Public Data and License Notes

## OpenStreetMap

- Provider: OpenStreetMap contributors.
- License: Open Database License (ODbL) 1.0.
- Intended OSIT use: street/path/rail topology and public map tags retrieved through OSMnx/Overpass/Nominatim.
- Requirement: preserve attribution and comply with the ODbL and upstream service usage policies.

## Optional future sources

Municipal open-data portals, GTFS feeds, public census aggregates, public elevation models, flood-risk layers, and openly licensed land-cover products may be integrated only after recording their authoritative source URL, license, retrieval timestamp, geographic scope, CRS, query parameters, checksum where available, and quality notes.

OSIT must never silently replace a failed source with another dataset.
