ClearGlass Intelligence Platform

One brand architecture for the whole ecosystem — from a handful of agents to hundreds.

Why an architecture, not a list of bot names? A coherent taxonomy makes every product line, platform tier, and autonomous agent feel like one operating system. Each name has a place, a prefix, and an honest status: operational names are bound to a system that already ships in this repo; reserved names hold their place in the namespace but are not yet provisioned. The source of truth is data/platform/architecture.json, validated by scripts/platform_registry.py.
