Questions, answered

How do I pay?

Three ways, your choice: Interac e-Transfer (no fees) to desmondotieno@icloud.com, secure card checkout where enabled, or a confirmed invoice. Nothing is auto-charged.

What do I get, and how fast?

Each engagement has fixed, written deliverables — e.g. the Quick-Audit returns a risk-ranked findings report in 3–5 business days plus a 30-minute readout call.

Will anything change on my systems?

The Quick-Audit is strictly read-only. Hands-on work (e.g. the Hardening Sprint) proceeds only under your written authorization, with changes applied on your sign-off.
